## Getting in (2 minutes)

1. Open the link and pick the right door: **Artist** if you're testing as a producer, **Curator** if you're testing as a video creator.
2. Sign up with **email + password** (Google sign-in is disabled on the alpha).
3. ⚠️ **Remember your password.** Password-reset emails don't send on the alpha — if you get locked out, you'll need to register a fresh account.
4. Curators: fill out your profile (bio, platforms, genres) so you show up in Discover.

## What to test — the full loop

- **Artist:** create a campaign for a track, find the curator in Discover, send an invite.
- **Curator:** accept the invite from your invite inbox (declining costs nothing; accepting uses one of your monthly invite credits).
- **Both:** chat in the collaboration workspace — try uploading a draft video (MP4/WebM/MOV), request changes with a reason, approve, then submit a live link as proof of posting.
- **Both:** request a resolve and confirm it from the other side; leave each other a star review.
- **Both:** click the notification bell as things happen — tell us if anything you expected never showed up.

## Please ignore

- The **role switcher** in the profile menu and the **"Preview as"** toggle in workspaces — those are Eyal's own testing tools. You can peek through them to see the other side, but judge the app from your real account.
- Any **demo curator** profiles — test accounts, not real people.
- Anything about payments or subscriptions — real payments aren't connected yet. The plan (artist pays upfront, Sync holds it, curator gets paid on proof-of-posting) is documented in the FAQ, but nothing will actually charge you.
